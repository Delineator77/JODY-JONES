A · The space as currently built measured from canyon.js

One screen of battlefield: roughly 90 units wide × 30 deep of playable staging, capped left and right by rock buttresses. Jody is fixed behind cover — a standoff arena, not a corridor. Aim is clamped to the ivory sight cone.

The honest finding: the current space is a wide, shallow theater stage — Jody cannot move, and the canyon is only ~30 units deep before the far wall. Your instinct is right: the brief describes a long, slightly curved corridor, and advancing along it is the game. That means restaging the long axis along the river, as below.

B · Proposed traversal layout — "The Run" long & skinny · ~340 × 46 units

Jody advances west → east down the canyon, cover to cover along the south bank, crossing the river twice. The river always runs beside you; the walls always tower on both flanks. Enemies hold the north bank in staggered pockets, and Crow's overwatch ledge controls the Narrows — the set-piece of the run.

  1. Start — gravel spit. Horses tucked in the alcove behind. First shots ring out as Jody pushes off.
  2. Cover run. Boulder chain on the south bank; pocket of 2 outlaws across the water teaches the peek/fire rhythm.
  3. Ford 1. Shallow crossing — exposed, bullet splashes chasing. First real pressure.
  4. North-bank maze. Fighting through their side of the river: 3 outlaws at close quarters among big rocks.
  5. The Narrows. Canyon pinches to ~18 units, walls overhang, and Crow's Sharps controls the gap from his ledge — move only between thunderclaps. The set-piece.
  6. Ford 2. Back across to the south spit under fire from the last pocket.
  7. Final bar — the goal. Last stand at the east bend; clearing it advances the story (and the canyon opens toward the next scene).

What implementing "The Run" needs: restage the built geometry along the river's axis (banks, wall spines, cover chains from the existing organic builders) · give Jody actual movement along the corridor (the FPS rig is currently locked in place — WASD today only nudges aim) · zone triggers that wake each enemy pocket · Crow's overwatch beat in the Narrows. All the visual systems (cliffs, water, characters, lighting, grade) carry over unchanged.
