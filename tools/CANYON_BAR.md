# The Bar — Canyon River Shootout

Judge the rendered frames against THIS, and nothing else. The bar is deliberately out
of reach; the job is to find the single biggest gap, not to be kind.

## The Core Visual (the client's own words)

> First-person view from behind a huge dark river boulder. Jody's Colt appears at the
> bottom of frame. A rushing blue river crosses the center of the image. Five outlaw
> gunmen hide behind rocks on the opposite bank, firing across the water. Orange muzzle
> flashes illuminate their silhouettes. Harlan Crow stands farther back with his Sharps
> rifle. Enormous midnight-blue canyon walls rise around the battlefield while their
> upper edges burn orange in the setting sun. Bullet impacts kick water into the air as
> smoke drifts through the canyon.

## Required qualities

- **Style target:** "a Sergio Leone gunfight you can stand inside" — an *illustrated
  cinema* look: a real 3D render (true light, shadow, depth, relief) finished as a
  graphic novel (flat colour masses, navy ink linework, bold silhouettes).
  It must NOT read as "Call of Duty in Monument Valley", and must NOT read as cheap
  flat low-poly programmer art either.
- **Palette (locked):** Midnight Navy `#14203a`, Burnt Vermillion `#b04a29`, Warm Ivory
  `#ecdfc4`, Olive Green `#555c37`; accents Aged Brass `#a87b3e`, Stone Gray `#6b6a66`.
- **Production rules:** bold simple shapes; strong graphic silhouettes at all distances;
  limit fine detail, no tiny parts; use colour to define planes and depth; illustrated
  cinema throughout.
- **Colour separation:** lower canyon in deep cool shadow; upper canyon walls burning
  warm; the player is IN shadow. Gunfire is the only warm light down in the dark.
- **The river is the signature:** a reflection streak running toward the viewer (amber
  at dusk, silver at night) over deep navy water.
- **Scale:** the canyon should feel enormous and enclosing — the player feels physically
  trapped at the bottom of a giant stone corridor.
- **Depth:** clear foreground / midground / background separation.
- **Enemies:** never exposed like shooting-gallery targets; read by hat, silhouette,
  movement, muzzle flash. Crow is unmistakable and distinct from the hired guns.

## How to judge

Look at the actual pixels in the PNGs you are given. Do not assume anything about how
they were made. Name the SINGLE biggest gap between what you see and the bar above,
be specific about where in the frame it is, and say concretely what should change.
Also give a 1–10 score for how close the frame is to the bar.
